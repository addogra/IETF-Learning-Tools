
# PRODUCT SENSE

Users want low-friction answers from noisy standards data.

Prioritize:
1. Relevance first: return the right WG/work item before returning large output.
2. Reliability second: deterministic parsing and graceful fallback behavior.
3. Time-to-value third: minimal prompt burden, fast useful output.

Product north star:
- A new engineer can describe a technology area and quickly find the right WG.
- An experienced engineer can query a WG and receive actionable summaries for drafts, discussions, and meetings.

Current product tension:
- The implementation is strong for WG-known flows.
- The technology-onboarding/vector matching flow is still a major gap and should remain a top roadmap item.
